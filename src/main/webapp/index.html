ABOUT VISWA GRID

Building Better Communities Through Smart Solutions.

VISWA GRID is a Smart Civic Issue Reporting and Resolution System designed to bridge the gap between citizens and civic authorities.

Our platform provides a centralized environment where citizens can report civic problems, track complaint progress and stay connected with the authorities responsible for maintaining public infrastructure.

Our vision is to encourage transparency, accountability and active citizen participation in creating cleaner and safer communities.
